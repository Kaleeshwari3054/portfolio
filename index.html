About Me

Hello, I'm Kaleeshwari from Chennai. My mother's name is Muthukali. She works as an ironer and inspires me with her hard work.

I completed my 12th grade at Avichi Higher Secondary School. After school, I joined Meenakshi College for a BCA degree and have completed two years of the program before deciding to focus on enhancing my technical abilities.

I recently completed my training at Freshworks Software Academy, where I improved my technical and problem-solving skills. I enjoy building projects from scratch, which led me to create a personal portfolio, an OTT website, and a music streaming app. I’m passionate about the MERN stack and have hands-on experience with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React JS, and currently learning Node.js and Express.js.

My hobbies include building side projects using the MERN stack, exploring new frontend tools, solving coding challenges, and learning about UI/UX design trends.
